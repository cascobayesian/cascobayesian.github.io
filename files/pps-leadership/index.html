Schools

Principals report to an assistant superintendent; assistant principals report to their principal. Principal and assistant principal pay follows the Portland Administrators Association contract (2025–26 principals: $107,241–$135,968 depending on level and step).

Who arrived when

Everyone at director level and above: directors, senior and executive directors, the controller, chiefs, assistant superintendents and the superintendent. A filled dot marks when the person joined Portland Public Schools; a ring marks when they took their current role. Dates come from Board personnel reports, district announcements and school pages. Board dates are appointment dates, so a start may fall a few weeks later.

Budget vs. salaries

The FY27 budget (adopted April 14, 2026) has no single line for administrator pay. Administrators are folded into each department's “Regular Salaries” line and into a district-wide “Administration/Regular Support” category ($25.5M) that also covers secretaries, custodians and other support staff. The comparisons below line up the salaries found on Board personnel reports with the budget lines that pay for them.

How to read this

  • Reporting lines come from the district's “Who Does What” org chart (updated September 2026). Older district pages still place special education under the Chief of Strategy & Operations; this map follows the newer chart, which puts it under the Chief of Schools & Academics.
  • Joined gives the start date in the current role and, where known, when the person first joined the district.
  • Salary at hire is the annual salary listed on Board of Public Education personnel reports when the person was appointed. It is not current pay and excludes benefits. Blank means no published figure was found.
  • Education is marked Verified when stated in a district bio, school page, news release or university page, and Unconfirmed when it was seen only in a search-result excerpt of a professional profile.

Gaps

  • Several directors have no published education or start date; the Board's personnel minutes are the best place to fill these in.
  • Board personnel reports with salaries are online from fall 2024 on, with a gap from February to August 2025.
  • The superintendent's 2025 contract renewal was approved after executive session; no salary or term was published.
  • A tentative 2026–29 principals' contract was on the Oct 27, 2026 Board agenda and was not yet ratified.
